# How we work

1. Pull `main` and read `docs/CONTEXT.md`.
2. Claim your task in `docs/CONTEXT.md` under "In progress".
3. Work on a branch: `<github-login>/<task>`.
4. Before stopping, update `docs/CONTEXT.md` and open a pull request.
5. The other person reviews and merges.

Both of our Claudes follow the same steps, written out in `CLAUDE.md`.
